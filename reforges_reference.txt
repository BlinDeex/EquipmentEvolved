MELEE WEAPON REFORGES (11)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ULTRA LIGHT
  Stats: -15% size, 2× swing speed, -35% damage
  Grants auto-reuse. Halves NPC hit cooldown on all spawned projectiles
  (switches to localNPCImmunity system if needed).

GIGANTIC
  Stats: +75% size, -15% swing speed
  All spawned projectiles also scale +75% size with corrected center position.

TITAN FORCE
  Stats: ×5 knockback, +25% damage

ARCANE INFUSED
  Stats: +85% damage
  Every swing costs 10 mana (blocks swing if insufficient, triggers 300t regen delay).
  While ManaSickness debuff is on the PLAYER: damage.Flat is overwritten proportionally
  to buff time remaining (effectively cancels the bonus while sick).

KINETIC
  Stats: -20% damage
  Damage multiplier = 1 + (playerVelocity.Length × 0.25), capped at +250% bonus.
  Standing still gives no bonus (net -20%). Updates every frame.

UNTOUCHABLE
  Stats: (dynamic, see below)
  Stacks +damage linearly over 15s while held without being hit. Max stack = 2.5 (250% bonus).
  At max stack: weapon swing animation halved (useTime unchanged), eye glow visual.
  Player takes (1 + currentStack) damage multiplier — at max, player takes 3.5× damage.
  Any hit resets stack to 0 instantly.

GRAVITIC
  On every swing frame, pulls all nearby non-boss hostile NPCs toward the player.
  Pull radius: 100 + (useTime × 8), capped at 800px. Respects NPC knockback resistance.
  Screen distortion shader active during swing.

SUNDERING
  Stats: -50% damage
  Each hit adds (targetDefense × 0.02) to a per-NPC armor penetration counter.
  On every incoming hit to that NPC (from anyone), all accumulated penetration applies.
  No decay — permanent stacking per NPC for their lifetime.

PERCEPTIVE
  Stats: +50 crit chance, -10% damage
  On crit hit, if total weapon crit > 100%: each 100% over threshold rolls a cascade tier
  (50% chance each, up to 6 tiers). Tier 1: +1.0× damage. Each extra tier: +0.5×.
  Custom combat text (orange→red by tier). Vanilla combat text hidden.

PAYBACK
  On melee hit: consumes 1–3 coins from inventory (lowest slots first), spawns them as
  homing projectiles. Damage = hit damage × coin tier (copper 0.10×, silver 0.40×,
  gold 1.0×, platinum 4.0×). Coins fall 45t then home to target. 95% chance to return
  as pickup on death.

SEALED
  On application: generates 1–10 hidden stat rolls (weighted RNG).
  Stat pool: CharmLuck, CritDamage, Damage, Regen, Lifesteal, Iframes.
  Roll count: 30% for 1, down to 0.2% for 10. Strength multiplier: 1× to 10× (weighted).
  Right-click to reveal stats — permanently locks the item (cannot be reforged again).

RANGED WEAPON REFORGES (10)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ADAPTABLE
  Stats: +20% slower fire rate
  Allows any ammo type in any ranged weapon. When a non-rocket weapon fires rocket ammo,
  kills the spawned projectile and replaces it with the proper rocket type (full 12-type
  lookup table). Rocket weapons are excluded from the swap.

CONTROLLED
  Stats: massively faster fire rate during burst window (×0.15 use time)
  Burst 12 ticks, cooldown 35 ticks (~0.8s cycle). During cooldown CanUseItem = false.
  Projectile velocity ×1.75. Only applicable to auto-reuse weapons.

VAMPIRIC
  Stats: +25% slower fire rate, +0.5 lifesteal (heals ~0.5 HP per hit, fractional RNG)
  Lifesteal applied through the stat system; healing boosted by HealingMulStat.

GIANT SLAYER  [Experimental]
  Converts all damage into true damage = 0.3% of the target's max HP per hit.
  The normal hit is cancelled (set to 1 damage, no crit). True damage bypasses defense.
  White "true damage" combat text. Skips target dummies outside dev mode.

RICOCHET
  On tile collision: projectile dies, spawns 2 copies in a 30° spread.
  Each split: ×0.70 damage, ×0.75 scale. Up to 2 split generations (4 final projectiles).
  Children inherit compounded multipliers.

THAUMIC
  On projectile hit: applies a random positive vanilla buff to the PLAYER.
  If player is missing any valid buffs, applies one for 5s base. If all valid buffs active,
  extends a random one by 3s. Hard cap 30s per buff.
  Buff pool built at startup (filters debuffs, pets, persistent, blacklisted IDs).

DELAYED  [Experimental]
  On spawn, projectile freezes in place, stores velocity + traces endpoint 3000px ahead.
  Every 180 ticks while firing, or when firing stops: all frozen projectiles simultaneously
  fire a laser (10px width, 0.33× damage, 15-frame lifespan, infinite penetration).
  Preview line drawn from projectile to endpoint while frozen.

ASCENDANT
  Tracks cumulative damage dealt. Threshold = weaponDamage × (50/useTime) × 1000.
  As damage approaches threshold: scales linearly to +190% damage, +40 crit.
  At threshold: weapon permanently burns out (unusable, grayed in inventory).
  Reforging resets the burnout and counters.

CHALLENGER  [Experimental, minigame reforge]
  Spawns color-coded orbs in a 90° arc behind the player (relative to mouse), 50–100px away.
  Score range 1–10. Mode activates at score ≥ 2, deactivates at ≤ 1.5.
  Orb timing window: frames 20–38 of 56 = "perfect" hit.
  Per-orb effects (stackable multipliers applied while mode active):
    Green:  +0.03 defense mult, +0.03 damage mult on player
    Blue:   +0.15 proj velocity mult, +0.05 crit mult on player
    Yellow: Green + Blue, heals player 15 HP
    Red:    resets all multipliers + score, deals 35 true damage to player
  Miss (orb expires): score ×0.75, all multipliers scale toward 1 by ×0.75 on excess.
  While active: player defense/damage/crit/velocity all multiplied by (score × multiplier).

FLOW STATE
  On hit: adds 1 stack + refreshes 60t timer on the target NPC.
  When timer expires, stacks decay 1 per 2 ticks.
  DoT per tick: (weaponDamage × 0.15 × stacks − npcDefense × 0.5) / 60, min 1 DPS.
  Displays stack count, accumulated damage, timer above the NPC.
  NPC life reduced directly (server-authoritative kill).

MAGIC WEAPON REFORGES (7)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ENDLESS
  Stats: 0% mana cost, -15% damage. No other mechanics.

MANA CHARGED  (called "Tidal" in design docs)
  Damage bonus scales with current mana %: up to +100% base damage at full mana, 0 at empty.
  Tooltip shows live value.

INVERTED  (Mana Surge)
  While holding the weapon, vanilla ManaSickness is replaced with InvertedBuff (custom).
  InvertedBuff: adds up to +125% magic damage scaling with remaining time (max 600t / 10s).
  Each reapplication extends by 300t capped at 600t.
  While buff active: 97.5% chance per tick to deal 15 true damage to the PLAYER (self-hurt).
  On death from this: death cancelled (once). Explodes outward — 10 nodes × 10–20 projectiles
  (friendly, 20 dmg each, 999 penetrate), plays explosion sound. One-time only.

TRIPLE SHOT
  Stats: -40% damage, ×3.5 mana cost
  Every projectile spawns 2 clones at ±10° (±5° random variation). 3 total per cast.
  Clones are flagged to prevent recursion.

SPLINTERING
  30% chance on projectile hit to spawn 1–4 shard nodes inside the target.
  On crit: node count ×2. Each node fires 1–3 shards (friendly magic, 5× extra updates,
  120t lifespan, cannot hit the original target).

CHAOTIC
  Every 30t while held (or in inventory), re-rolls a bundle of 1–5 stats from a weighted pool.
  Rarities: Common (10w), Rare (5w), Epic (3w), Legendary (1.5w), Negative (9w).
  Negative rolls flip all bonuses into drawbacks (×-1.2 intensity).
  Stats applied to the PLAYER while the weapon is held. Tooltip shows current roll with color.
  Also displays a random "faked" magic prefix label — that prefix's mechanic is NOT applied.

OVERLOADED  [Experimental]
  Forces weapon into channel mode. While held: drains mana every 10t at increasing rate
  (+1× cost per second of charging). Fires on release or when mana runs out.
  Output damage: ×2.0 max scaling based on total mana consumed. Scale grows up to ×4.0.
  Visual: expanding dust ring while charging.

SUMMONER — MINION WEAPON REFORGES (3)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FRENZIED
  Spawned minions run 4× AI updates per tick (via a detour on Projectile.AI).
  Effectively quadruples minion movement and attack speed.

MONARCH
  When a new minion of the same type is summoned while one already exists:
  if combined slots fit within maxMinions, the new minion is absorbed into the existing one
  (MonarchStacks++, slots grow). Otherwise the new one spawns normally.
  Stat scaling from stacks (soft cap 10, diminishing returns at 0.2× above):
    Size:   0.7 + (stacks-1) × 0.68 per stack
    Damage: effectiveStacks × (1 + (effectiveStacks-1) × 0.35) — roughly quadratic
  Child projectiles from the minion inherit the damage multiplier.
  On death: if same type respawned within 1 tick, stacks partially restored.
  Cannot roll on StormTigerStaff, StardustDragonStaff, AbigailsFlower.

PROXY
  Spawned minion is anchored stationary at a random point 120px from summon location.
  Faces player's mouse direction. Lasts 600t (10s), then despawns with smoke.
  Duration bar displayed below the minion.
  While the proxy exists: whenever the PLAYER fires any weapon, a copy of that projectile
  spawns from the proxy's position aimed toward the mouse, at 60% combined damage
  (originalDamage + minionDamage) × 0.60. The proxy itself does not attack independently.
  Cannot roll on StormTigerStaff, StardustDragonStaff, AbigailsFlower.

DETONATING
  Stats: +15% slower use speed
  To rig a minion: the whip's hitbox must physically collide with the minion during a swing.
  Rigged minion shows a pulsing bomb overlay. Detonates when it hits an NPC, or after 300t (5s).
  Explosion: 3× weapon damage, 120px AoE (Summon type). Smoke + fire dust, explosion sound.

SACRIFICIAL
  Stats: massively faster whip (use time ×0.15, ~6.7× speed)
  On any hit: applies SacrificialBuff (non-removable self-debuff) to the PLAYER for 300t (5s),
  if not on cooldown.
  While buff active: next fatal hit is cancelled instead — grants iframes equal to
  (numMinions × 30 ticks), then ALL minions are dismissed. Buff removed.
  Cooldown after trigger: 3600t (60s).

GEOLOGICAL  [Pickaxe]
  5% chance per tile broken (Dirt or Stone only) to drop a random gem at that location.
  Gem pool: Amethyst, Topaz, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, Amber.

REVEALING  [Pickaxe]
  25% chance per tile broken to apply Spelunker buff to the PLAYER for 20 ticks.
  Also sets a 30-tick internal timer used for brightness visuals.

VEIN MINER  [Pickaxe]
  Stats: +25% slower swing
  When an ore or gem tile is broken: flood-fills and breaks all connected tiles of the same
  type within pickaxe power range. Entire veins cleared in one swing.

CLEARING  [Pickaxe / Hammer]
  Breaks a 3×3 area around the broken tile.
  60% chance per extra tile to destroy the item drop (not dropped, just lost).
  Will not destroy chests, dressers, demon altars, or tree trunks.

FORTUNE  [Axe]
  While swinging, activates a 30-tick Fortune window.
  Any item drop within 1000px has a 75% chance for bonus stacks (1–4 extra, best of 2 rolls).
  Drops are flagged to prevent re-processing.

ARMOR REFORGES — UNIVERSAL (any slot, set bonuses at 3 pieces)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CHRONO
  Per piece: +8% move speed (self).
  Set (3 pieces): Keybind (R) — freezes all hostile NPCs and projectiles within ~632px
  for 420t (7s). NPCs locked in position, cannot move or hit player.
  Projectiles paused (no movement, no damage). Cooldown: 3600t (60s) on the player.

PHANTOM
  No stat changes in code. [BUG: tooltip claims +15% true damage but it is never applied]
  Set (3 pieces): Keybind (R) toggles clones.
    Summoning clones (no clones active): spawns 2 orbiting clones (6 with augmentation).
    Clones mirror the player's held weapon — they physically run ItemCheck() at their position,
    firing copies of whatever the player fires from their orbit location. No cooldown on summon.
    Dismissing clones (clones active): kills all clones. Cooldown: 1200t (20s) on player.
  Self-debuffs while clones are active (applied to the PLAYER each tick, not enemies):
    VoidSealBuff: blocks use of healing items, clamps HP/mana preventing regen ticks.
    SoulBurnBuff: accumulates up to 1200t. Scales screen shader + distorts all sounds
    (lower volume, lower pitch, more variance at high intensity).
  When clones are dismissed: SoulBurn decays 5t/tick (faster recovery).

PHALANX
  Per piece: +8% damage (self).
  Set (3 pieces): When a hostile projectile hits the player AND (damage ≥ 10 OR hit is lethal):
    Negates the hit (CanBeHitByProjectile returns false).
    Grants 30t iframes to the PLAYER.
    Fires 15 plating projectiles from the impact point (8 side, 3 forward, 4 shockwave).
    Each plating projectile: 100 damage, friendly, on hit applies Burning debuff to the ENEMY (3s).
    Cooldown: 1800t (30s) on the player.
    With PhalanxOverclock augmentation: cooldown skipped, temporary overclock stat applied instead.

SYMBIOTIC
  No direct stat changes.
  Set (3 pieces): Upgrades charms socketed into all 3 Symbiotic armor pieces each tick:
    Bumps charm rarity +1 (capped at Exalted). Recalculates stat rolls upward. Adds 1 new roll.
    Augmentations consumed for additional quality bonus.
  Reverts when set falls below 3 pieces. Original charm data stored in snapshot.
  Note: 3× Symbiotic + all Exalted charms = condition for Exalted charm drops.

CONDUIT
  Per piece: +40 max mana (self).
  Set (3 pieces): Replaces vanilla mana regen entirely.
  Custom regen: constant 1 mana/tick (60 mana/s), accumulator-based. Vanilla regen zeroed.

AUGMENTED
  Per piece: grants vanilla buffs to the PLAYER based on that piece's defense value:
    Helmet  — Hunter (≥5 def), WellFed3 (≥10), Lifeforce (≥15)
    Chest   — Ironskin (≥5), Wrath (≥10), Endurance (≥15)
    Legs    — Swiftness (≥5), Rage (≥10), Gravitation (≥15)
  Buffs reapplied every tick (2t duration = always active while defense threshold met).
  Set (3 pieces): passive ability — adds +2% damage per non-debuff buff currently active
  on the player. Suppressed while armor ability cooldown buff is on the player.

CURSED
  Per piece: +0.25 lifesteal (self).
  Set (3 pieces): modifies incoming damage TO the player:
    If damage > 5% of player maxHP: caps damage to 80% of maxHP (near-lethal but survivable).
    If damage ≤ 5% of maxHP: hit fully cancelled, 20t iframes, "Negated!" text shown.
    With CursedAugmentation: thresholds shift to 20% (cancel) / 60% max taken.

RAID BOSS
  Per piece: +40% of that piece's own base defense as flat extra defense (self).
  Set (3 pieces): self stat changes —
    -50% move speed, -50% wing horizontal/vertical speed.
    +25 flat defense, +15% damage reduction, +1000 aggro.
  Full tank — nearly immobile, draws all enemy attention, extremely durable.

INEVITABLE  [WIP / Legacy]
  Per piece: +8% generic damage (self, applied directly each tick).
  Set (3 pieces): Keybind (R) starts a 720t (12s) countdown on the player.
  During countdown: cyan dust ring around player, tick sounds every second.
  At countdown end — ExecuteWipe: hits all enemies within 2000px:
    Boss: 10% of boss maxHP. Non-boss: maxHP × 5 (one-shots).
    Bypasses normal damage calculations via StrikeNPCDirect.
  Cooldown: 10800t (3 min) starts at activation (not at wipe).

  At 5 stacks — SHATTER: defense penalty equal to the piece's base defense for 600t (10s).

VITALIS

  Disabled while armor ability cooldown is active.

VOID

  Disabled while armor ability cooldown is active.

UNYIELDING
  Damage reduction scales with missing HP: up to +20% DR at 0 HP, 0 at full HP (self).

CHARM SYSTEM NOTES (for synergy context)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Charms are RNG items socketed into armor. Rarities: Common < Rare < Epic < Legendary < Mythical < Exalted.
Exalted only drops when wearing 3× Symbiotic armor all socketed with Exalted charms.

Symbiotic (armor) upgrades socketed charms: +1 rarity, stronger stats, extra roll.
CharmLuckStat (from Lucky acc, Sealed weapon, etc.) improves drop rates.
Pity system: LegendaryPity and MythicalPity tracked per player, guaranteed drop at threshold.

Augmentations on Legendary+ charms (implemented):
  Clearing:       expands AoE mining radius
  Cursed:         shifts Cursed armor thresholds (cancel at 20%, cap at 60%)
  Phalanx:        overclock — reflections ignore cooldown, use 2s overclock stat instead
  Phantom:        increases clone count from 2 to 6, ramps SoulBurn intensity faster
  Omni:           endgame augmentation, Exalted Circle charms only, chance-gated
