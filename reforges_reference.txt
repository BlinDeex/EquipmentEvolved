MELEE WEAPON REFORGES (11)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ULTRA LIGHT
  Stats: -20% size, -20% use time, -20% damage
  Grants auto-reuse. Reduces NPC hit cooldown by 20% on all projectiles
  (uses localNPCImmunity system if weapon doesn't use static immunity).

GIGANTIC
  Stats: +50% size, -20% use time (faster swing, bigger weapon)
  All spawned projectiles scale to +50% size with corrected center position.

TITAN FORCE
  Stats: +100% knockback, -10% damage
  Pure knockback amplifier. No special logic.

ARCANE INFUSED
  Stats: -5% damage
  Every swing costs 10 mana and triggers 300-tick mana regen delay.
  While ManaSickness buff is active: damage is further reduced
  proportionally to buff duration remaining (scales from 0 up to -10% at max).

KINETIC
  Stats: -25% base damage
  Damage multiplier = 1 + (playerVelocity * 0.1), capped at +250% bonus.
  Faster you're moving, harder you hit. Updates every frame.

UNTOUCHABLE
  Stats: damage taken multiplied by (1 + currentStack)
  Passively stacks +0.01 damage increase per frame while held without being hit.
  Stack cap: 1.0 (at max: weapon swing speed doubled, eye glow visual).
  Any damage received resets stack to 0 immediately.

GRAVITIC
  On every swing, pulls all nearby hostile NPCs toward the player.
  Pull radius: 150 + (useTime * 4), capped at 800. Pull speed: 8 * (0.95^useTime).
  Respects NPC knockback resistance. Screen distortion shader during swing.

SUNDERING
  Stats: -15% damage
  Each hit adds (targetDefense * 0.2) to a per-NPC shred counter.
  On the next hit, all accumulated shred applies as armor penetration.
  Shred persists between hits on the same NPC. Synced in multiplayer.

PERCEPTIVE
  Stats: +10% crit chance, +15% damage
  Activates only when total crit chance exceeds 100%.
  Excess crit checked in 100% tiers — each tier has 50% chance to add damage:
    Tier 1: +100% damage multiplier
    Tier 2+: +50% each, up to tier 3 maximum (2.0x total multiplier cap)
  Hides vanilla combat text, shows custom damage number with tier indicator.
  Synced over network for visual effect.

PAYBACK
  On any melee hit, spawns 3–5 coins as homing projectiles.
  Coins fall and drift for 45 ticks, then home to the target NPC at speed 24.
  Damage per coin = hit damage * coin multiplier (copper 0.3x → platinum 1.0x).
  20% chance each coin returns as a pickup item on death.

SEALED
  Generates 1–10 hidden stat rolls on application (weighted RNG).
  Roll count: 30% chance for 1 roll, 25% for 2 ... down to 0.2% for 10.
  Strength multiplier per roll: 30% at 1.0x up to 0.2% at 10.0x.
  Possible stats: CharmLuck, CritDamage, Damage, Regen, Lifesteal, Iframes.
  Right-click to reveal stats. Once revealed, cannot be reforged (locked).
  Stats persist through save/load and multiplayer sync.

RANGED WEAPON REFORGES (10)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ADAPTABLE
  Stats: -10% use time (faster fire)
  Allows all ammo types regardless of weapon restrictions.
  Converts rocket ammo fired from non-rocket weapons into proper rocket projectiles
  (full lookup table of 12 rocket types).

CONTROLLED
  Stats: -15% use time, +25% projectile velocity
  Alternates between a 180-tick burst window and a 120-tick cooldown.
  During cooldown, ammo selection is blocked — weapon cannot fire.
  Only applicable to auto-reuse weapons.

VAMPIRIC
  Stats: -10% use time
  Adds 8% lifesteal via LifeStealStat (works through the stat system).

GIANT SLAYER  [Experimental]
  Adds 15% true damage (bypasses defense) via TrueDamagePercentageStat.
  Flagged experimental; interacts with FullHitCancellation bypass logic.

RICOCHET
  On tile collision, projectile splits into 2 new projectiles (±22.5° spread).
  Each split: -25% damage, -10% scale. Up to 3 splits per projectile chain.
  Children inherit compounded multipliers from parents (damage compounds).

THAUMIC
  On projectile hit: applies a random positive buff from the vanilla buff pool.
  Base duration: 300 ticks (5s). Each additional hit extends by 60 ticks, cap 3600.
  If buff is already active, extends it instead of reapplying.
  Buff pool is dynamically built at startup (filters out debuffs, pets, blacklisted buffs).

DELAYED
  Projectiles freeze in place on spawn, storing velocity and a laser endpoint
  (scanned 3000 units forward to find solid geometry).
  Every 60 ticks while firing, or on release: all frozen projectiles simultaneously
  fire a laser beam (80% damage, 15-tick duration, penetrating) at their stored endpoints.
  Visual: cyan line drawn from projectile to endpoint during charge phase.

ASCENDANT
  Tracks cumulative damage dealt. As damage approaches the threshold (weapon damage × 40),
  bonuses scale linearly from 0 to max:
    +150% damage, +30 crit chance at full charge.
  At threshold: weapon burns out and becomes unusable (grayed out in inventory).
  Reforging the weapon resets the burn state and counters.

CHALLENGER  [Minigame reforge]
  Spawns color-coded orbs behind the player (90° cone opposite mouse direction).
  Orb spawn rate accelerates with score. Orbs have a perfect hit window (frames 20–38).
  Orb types and effects (stackable):
    Green:  +0.15 defense multiplier, +0.10 damage multiplier
    Blue:   +0.25 projectile velocity multiplier, +0.05 crit multiplier
    Yellow: Green + Blue combined, +heals player (VampireHeal projectile)
    Red:    Resets ALL bonuses, deals 50 damage to player
  Score range: 1.0 to 10.0.
  Mode activates at score ≥ 5.0, deactivates at ≤ 3.0.
  While active: applies all accumulated multipliers to weapon stats and defense.
  Score synced over network.

FLOW STATE
  On hit, applies a stacking DoT debuff to the enemy.
  Stack duration: 180 ticks per hit (resets on new hit). Stacks decay at 1/120 ticks.
  Damage per tick: (projectileDamage × 0.25 × stacks) - (defense × 0.5), minimum 1.
  Fractional damage accumulates between ticks.
  Displays real-time stack count, damage, and timer above affected enemy.
  Applies damage directly to NPC.life (server-authoritative).

MAGIC WEAPON REFORGES (7)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ENDLESS
  Stats: -15% damage, 0% mana cost (free casting)
  Removes mana cost entirely. No other mechanics.

MANA CHARGED  (listed as "Tidal" in design docs)
  Damage scales with current mana percentage.
  Bonus = (currentMana / maxMana) × maxBonusMultiplier, max +100% damage at full mana.
  Tooltip shows dynamic damage value.

INVERTED  (Mana Surge)
  Replaces vanilla ManaSickness with a custom InvertedBuff.
  Buff stacks up to 600 ticks; each reapplication extends by 300 ticks.
  While buff is active: magic damage bonus scales up to +125% at 600 ticks.
  Each frame with buff: 2.5% chance to deal 15 true damage to self.
  On death while buff is active: death is cancelled once. Player explodes outward
  with 10 burst nodes × 10–20 projectiles each (2000–6000 damage), one-time only.

TRIPLE SHOT
  Stats: -40% damage, ×3.5 mana cost
  Every projectile fired spawns 2 clones at ±10° (±5° random variation).
  3 total projectiles per cast, all with identical AI parameters.

SPLINTERING
  30% chance on projectile hit to spawn 1–4 shard nodes.
  On crit: node count ×2.
  Each node spawns 1–2 shards. Shards: speed 10 (±4), 5× extraUpdates (fast),
  120-tick lifespan, cannot hit the original target, 4px size.

CHAOTIC
  Periodically re-rolls a set of stat bonuses/penalties while the weapon is held
  or in inventory. Rolls drawn from a weighted pool:
    Common (10w) / Rare (5w) / Epic (3w) / Legendary (1.5w) / Negative (9w)
  Current rolled effects shown dynamically in tooltip with color-coded rarity.

OVERLOADED  [Experimental]
  Forces weapon into channel mode (must hold fire button).
  While held: drains mana every 10 ticks at an increasing rate (+1.0× per second).
  Fires when button released or mana runs out.
  Damage scales with total mana consumed: up to 2.0× at maximum efficiency.
  Projectile scale also grows up to 4.0×. Visual: expanding dust ring while charging.

SUMMONER — MINION WEAPON REFORGES (3)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FRENZIED
  Marks spawned minions with a flag for external speed amplification.
  Full speed multiplier applied through the stat system when marked.

MONARCH
  Absorbs new summons of the same minion type into the existing one, stacking them.
  Each absorbed summon increments MonarchStacks (capped at player.maxMinions).
  Stacks scale stats:
    Size: 0.7 + (stacks - 1) × 0.68 (grows large quickly)
    Damage: effectiveStacks × (1 + (effectiveStacks - 1) × 0.35) — quadratic scaling
  Soft cap at 10 stacks; above that, diminishing returns at 0.2× rate.
  On death: if a new same-type minion spawns within 1 tick, stacks partially restored.
  Child projectiles from the minion inherit the scaled stats.
  Tooltip shows current Monarch stats live.

PROXY
  Spawned minion becomes stationary at a fixed location (120px random offset from summon point).
  Minion faces mouse direction and lasts 10 seconds (600 ticks), then despawns with smoke.
  While active: every re-summon fires a copy of the minion's attack at 60% damage
  toward mouse direction from the proxy's position instead of summoning new minions.
  Duration bar displayed below the proxy.
  Cannot be used with Storm Tiger Staff, Stardust Dragon Staff, or Abigail's Flower.

DETONATING
  Stats: +15% use speed (faster whip)
  Whip swing rigs touched minions as explosives.
  Rigged minion detonates after 5 seconds (300 ticks) or immediately when it hits an NPC.
  Explosion: 3× weapon damage, 120px radius AoE (Summon damage type), 30 smoke/fire dust.
  Visual during rigging: sparks, bomb overlay with scale pulse, red tint in final 30 ticks.

SACRIFICIAL
  Stats: drastically increased attack speed (×0.15 use time multiplier — very fast)
  On any hit: applies SacrificialBuff (non-removable debuff) for 5 seconds (300 ticks) if not on cooldown.
  While buff is active: the next fatal hit is cancelled.
    Instead: grants iframes = numMinions × 30 (0.5s per minion), then dismisses ALL minions.
    Buff removed. Cooldown: 60 seconds (3600 ticks).
  Trade: minions as a death shield, lost on trigger.

GEOLOGICAL  [Pickaxe]
  5% chance per tile broken (Dirt or Stone only) to drop a random gem at that location.
  Gem pool: Amethyst, Topaz, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, Amber. Drop synced in MP.

REVEALING  [Pickaxe]
  25% chance per tile broken to apply the Spelunker buff for 30 ticks.
  Highlights ore and gem deposits in the surrounding area briefly.

VEIN MINER  [Pickaxe]
  Slightly slower swing speed (+0.25 use time).
  When an ore or gem tile is broken, flood-fills and breaks all connected tiles of the
  same type (bounded by pickaxe power). Entire veins destroyed in one swing.

CLEARING  [Pickaxe / Hammer]
  Breaks a 3×3 area centered on the target tile.
  60% chance per extra tile to destroy the drop (item lost, not dropped).
  Will not destroy chests, dressers, demon altars, or tree trunks.

FORTUNE  [Axe]
  While held during swing, activates a 30-tick Fortune window.
  Within 1000px, any item spawned from tile breaks has a 75% chance for 1–4 bonus stacks
  (takes the better of two random rolls, max +4 per item). Marks drops to prevent re-processing.

ARMOR REFORGES — UNIVERSAL (any slot, set bonuses at 3 pieces)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CHRONO
  Per piece: +movement speed bonus (MoveSpeedStat).
  Set (3 pieces): Keybind ability — freezes all hostile NPCs and projectiles within
  CHRONO_ABILITY_RANGE for CHRONO_ABILITY_LENGTH ticks. Cooldown applies after.
  Sends packet in multiplayer; server applies freeze to targets.

PHANTOM
  Per piece: applies true damage amplification (PHANTOM_TRUE_DAMAGE_AMP).
  Spawns orbiting clone phantoms (count increased with PhantomAugmentation).
  Clones mirror the player's weapon, animation, and direction.
  Clones apply two debuffs to affected enemies:
    VoidSeal: prevents healing, caps regen.
    SoulBurn: stacks to 1200 ticks max, extends while clones are active.
  Ability: toggle clones on/off (keybind). Destroying clones returns no cooldown;
  spawning them costs full cooldown. Shader effect scales with SoulBurn intensity.

PHALANX
  Per piece: +PHALANX_DAMAGE_INCREASE to DamageStat.
  Set (3 pieces): When a projectile hits the player for more than PHALANX_MIN_DAMAGE
  AND more than player's current HP — triggers reaction:
    Grants 30 ticks immunity.
    Fires 15 counterburst projectiles (8 side, 3 forward, 4 shockwave).
    All deal 100 damage + Burning debuff.
  Cooldown: PHALANX_REACT_COOLDOWN_TICKS.
  PhalanxOverclock augmentation: ignores cooldown, applies temporary overclock stat instead.

SYMBIOTIC
  Per piece: increases charm quality (SYMBIOTIC_BASE_QUALITY_BOOST, tooltip only).
  Set (3 pieces): Upgrades all charms socketed in Symbiotic armor pieces:
    Bumps rarity up by 1 (capped at Exalted).
    Recalculates and increases all stat roll strengths.
    Adds 1 new stat roll.
    If charm had augmentations, consumes them for an additional quality bonus.
  Reverts upgrade when set falls below 3 pieces. Stores original snapshot for reversion.
  Note: 3× Symbiotic + all Exalted charms is the condition for Exalted charm drops.

CONDUIT
  Per piece: +CONDUIT_MANA_PER_PIECE to max mana.
  Set (3 pieces): Completely replaces vanilla mana regeneration with a custom system.
  Vanilla regen is disabled (delay set to 999, regen set to 0).
  Custom regen: +CONDUIT_SET_MANA_REGEN_PER_TICK per tick, applied from an accumulator.
  Effectively gives extremely fast and consistent mana regeneration.

AUGMENTED
  Per piece: checks armor defense against configured thresholds; grants specific vanilla buffs
  while defense meets/exceeds each threshold (continuous 2-tick reapplication).
  Different threshold tables for helmet, chestplate, leggings.
  Set (3 pieces): Ability — while not on cooldown, +AUGMENTED_SET_BONUS_DAMAGE_PER_BUFF
  damage bonus per currently active (non-debuff) buff on the player.
  Cooldown timer applies after activation.

CURSED
  Per piece: +CURSED_FLAT_LIFESTEAL to LifeStealStat.
  Set (3 pieces): Damage mitigation on hit:
    If incoming damage > CURSED_IGNORED_DAMAGE_PERCENT_THRESHOLD × maxHP:
      cap damage to CURSED_DAMAGE_TAKEN_PERCENT × maxHP (excess absorbed).
    If damage is below threshold:
      cancel damage entirely, grant 20 ticks immunity, display "Negated!" text.
  CursedAugmentation: shifts the thresholds to augmented values.

RAID BOSS
  Per piece: +RAID_BOSS_PIECE_DEFENSE_MULT defense multiplier.
  Set (3 pieces): Heavy penalties to mobility:
    -RAID_BOSS_MOBILITY_PENALTY to MoveSpeed, WingHorizontalSpeed, WingVerticalSpeed.
  Compensating bonuses:
    +RAID_BOSS_SET_DEFENSE_BONUS flat defense, +RAID_BOSS_SET_DR_BONUS damage reduction,
    +RAID_BOSS_SET_AGGRO aggro (enemies target you more).
  Full tank identity — can't move, but extremely durable and draws aggro.

INEVITABLE  [Legacy / WIP]
  Per piece: +INEVITABLE_PIECE_DAMAGE_MULT to Generic damage.
  Set (3 pieces): Ability — starts a countdown timer (INEVITABLE_CLOCK_TICKS).
  During countdown: cyan dust ring around player, tick sound every second.
  On timer end: ExecuteWipe.
    ExecuteWipe hits all enemies within INEVITABLE_RANGE:
      Boss: npc.lifeMax × INEVITABLE_BOSS_MAX_HP_DAMAGE_PERCENT
      Non-boss: npc.lifeMax × 5
    Bypasses normal damage calculations (StrikeNPCDirect).
  Cooldown: INEVITABLE_COOLDOWN_TICKS after activation.

ARMOR REFORGES — HEADWEAR ONLY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BERSERKER
  Damage scales with missing HP.
  Bonus = (missingHPPercent) × BERSERKER_MAX_DAMAGE_BONUS (applied to DamageStat).
  At full HP: no bonus. At 0 HP: max bonus.

ENRAGING
  Adds +ENRAGING_AGGRO_INCREASE to player.aggro.
  Enemies target you more aggressively.

REINFORCED
  Amplifies the helmet's own defense value: +REINFORCED_DEFENSE_AMP × helmDefense (FlatDefenseStat).
  Bonus disabled while ArmorAbilityCooldownBuff is active.

WEAK LINK
  The helmet itself loses defense: -(WEAK_LINK_THIS_DEFENSE_MULT - 1) × helmDefense.
  Chestplate gains: +(WEAK_LINK_OTHER_DEFENSE_MULT - 1) × chestDefense.
  Leggings gains: +(WEAK_LINK_OTHER_DEFENSE_MULT - 1) × legsDefense.
  Trades helmet defense to amplify the other two slots.

PHASING
  Grants iframes on movement abilities:
    Dash (dashDelay transitions to -1): grants PHASING_DASH_IFRAMES immunity.
    Teleport (position displacement > velocity + 32px threshold): grants PHASING_TELEPORT_IFRAMES.
  Only grants iframes if the new duration would exceed current immuneTime.

PSIONIC
  Multiplies all healing item effectiveness by PSIONIC_HEAL_BONUS_MULT.
    Applies to: Heart, CandyApple, CandyCane (HP items).
    Applies to: Star, SoulCake, SugarPlum (Mana items).
  Activates item magnet for hearts, mana stars, and treasure.

DASHING
  No stat changes.
  Keybind ability: dash toward mouse cursor at DASHING_VELOCITY (uses momentum if higher).
  Has DASHING_MAX_CHARGES charges. Each dash consumes 1 charge.
  Recharges at 1 charge per DASHING_RECHARGE_TICKS.
  During dash: velocity locked, gravity off, water ignored, no fall damage.
  Duration: DASHING_DURATION_TICKS per dash.

REBOUNDING
  Each time player is hurt (damage > 15% maxHP OR knockback > 4): +1 stack, spawn 5 crystal
  shrapnel projectiles (damage = statDefense × REBOUNDING_DEFENSE_SCALING).
  Per stack: +(REBOUNDING_DEFENSE_PER_STACK × stacks × chestDefense) defense.
  Stack duration: REBOUNDING_STACK_DURATION ticks (resets on new hit).
  At REBOUNDING_MAX_STACKS: Shatter — defense disabled for 600 ticks, stacks reset,
  shattering sound and glass particles.

VITALIS
  Multiplies all lifesteal healing received by VITALIS_LIFESTEAL_AMP.
  Bonus disabled while ArmorAbilityCooldownBuff is active.

VOID
  Adds +VOID_TRUE_DAMAGE_BONUS to TrueDamageMulStat (flat true damage multiplier).
  Bonus disabled while ArmorAbilityCooldownBuff is active.

UNYIELDING
  Applies damage reduction up to UNYIELDING_MAX_DR.
  (Exact implementation is in UI/tooltip layer — core mechanic straightforward DR stat.)

TERRA
  Adds (legsDefense × 1.25) as flat defense — but only while the player is grounded
  (velocity.Y == 0). No bonus in the air.

SILENT
  -500 aggro (AggroStat). Enemies are significantly less likely to target you.

DESPERATE
  Maximum movement speed bonus (+60% move speed). Always active.

RESONANT
  Amplifies all equipped accessory reforge stat values by ×1.33 (33% stronger).
  Multiplicative with all accessory bonuses.

QUANTUM
  Converts vanilla dashes into teleports.
  On dash input: teleports player 250px toward mouse (finds safe landing in spiral if blocked).
  Leaves an afterimage projectile at origin (damage = statDefense × 3.5).
  Afterimage waits 30 ticks, then homes back to player at speed 35, dissipates within 40px.
  Player exits teleport with speed = max(previous velocity, 15).
  Cooldown: 60 ticks.

CHARM SYSTEM NOTES (for synergy context)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Charms are RNG items socketed into armor pieces.
Rarities: Common < Rare < Epic < Legendary < Mythical < Exalted
Exalted charms only drop when wearing 3× Symbiotic armor all socketed with Exalted charms.

Symbiotic (armor reforge) upgrades socketed charms: +1 rarity, stronger stats, extra roll.
CharmLuckStat (from Lucky accessory, Sealed weapon, Equilibrium) improves charm drop rates.

Augmentations are extra modifiers on Legendary+ charms. Implemented ones:
  Clearing:  expands AoE radius
  Cursed:    shifts Cursed armor thresholds
  Phalanx:   adds Phalanx overclock (ignore cooldown, use overclock stat instead)
  Phantom:   increases clone count + modifies SoulBurn ramp
  Omni:      mystery endgame augmentation (chance-gated, Exalted Circle charms only)
